## KRE Security Vehicle Patrol Division

KRE provides a custom patrol service based on the client's needs and venue. Our fleet is fully insured and licensed for vehicle use at facility grounds. Each vehicle is configured and equipped to meet the operational demands of the client's site.

\[ Fleet Options \]

## Vehicle Fleet Overview

\[ Marked Fleet \]

### Marked Patrol Vehicles

High-visibility branded patrol vehicles providing a deterrent presence and active patrol of client facilities. Fully insured and licensed for facility grounds.

\[ Covert Fleet \]

### Unmarked / Covert Vehicles

Discreet, unmarked patrol vehicles for covert surveillance and undercover security operations — indistinguishable from standard civilian vehicles.

\[ All-Terrain \]

### 4x4 & All-Wheel Drive

4x4 and all-wheel drive capable vehicles can be stationed for the client's specific environmental needs, including off-road and challenging terrain environments.

\[ Venue Specific \]

### Security Patrol Golf Carts

Golf carts can be utilized on venue-specific areas, offering a cost savings for the facility. Available during warmer conditions and marked for visibility. Can reduce fuel expenses at facilities.
