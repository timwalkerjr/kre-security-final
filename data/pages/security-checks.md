## What Do Home Security Checks Include?

Check all doors and windows to be sure closed and locked

Water indoor and outdoor plants per your instructions

Bring in mail, solicitation flyers & newspapers

Rotate lights for lived-in look

Check for water leaks under sinks, toilets and washing machine

After rain storm check ceilings in home and garage for water leaks

Follow up with landscaper, pool server, housekeeper, pest control, etc., as needed

Spray weeds (client provides weed spray)

Put out trash cans on scheduled trash, then put away after emptied

Prepare home for client's return — reset thermostat, turn some lights on, schedule housekeeper, pest control, etc.

After each inspection, we will notify you of our findings

Alarm response by officers to check on your property while you are away
