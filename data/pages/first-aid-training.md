## Training Classes We Offer

KRE Security will provide the following classes — as approved by the [American Heart Association](https://www.heart.org/) and the [American Red Cross](https://www.redcross.org/) — offering customized certification programs for healthcare providers and lay rescuers, including any course in the AHA and BLS Family of courses curriculum.

- Heartsaver First Aid
- Heartsaver CPR/AED
- Friends and Family CPR
- BLS for the Healthcare Provider
- Heartsaver Bloodborne Pathogens
- Pediatric First Aid
