# Admissions Theft Found at a Local Event

KRE Security — Field ReportReading, PA

\[ Incident Report — Event Security, Reading PA \]

Admissions theft was found at a local event we were assigned to in Reading, PA, due to proper screening by the KRE Gate Team performing safety inspections and ID verification processes.

![KRE Security event gate team on duty at Reading PA event](https://pagesmith-cdn.com/fe6bcc20/images/63861c_868da01a17d442288157703867f3b154-mv2-320-331dae-320.webp)

We pride ourselves in the identification of anyone who may try to be fraudulent at our events. This successful intercept demonstrates the effectiveness of KRE Security's rigorous event security protocols — including multi-layer gate screening, comprehensive safety inspections, and thorough ID verification procedures.

\[ Gate Screening \]

Comprehensive entry screening for all attendees to ensure event integrity and public safety.

\[ ID Verification \]

Rigorous identity confirmation processes to prevent fraud and unauthorized entry at all controlled access points.

\[ Safety Inspections \]

Full safety inspections performed by trained KRE Gate Teams to maintain secure event perimeters.

KRE Security's event staffing services go beyond simple crowd management. Our professional Gate Teams are trained to identify fraudulent activity, enforce admission protocols, and maintain the overall security and integrity of every event we serve — from community gatherings to large-scale public events across Eastern Pennsylvania.

\[ Event Staffing Services \]

Protect your event with professional KRE Security event staffing, including gate teams, traffic control, and armed or unarmed security personnel throughout Eastern Pennsylvania.
