## Join the KRE Security Team

KRE Security LLC offers a supplemental benefits package with partial employer contribution to employees who average 30 hours or more per week. We are proud to support the officers who represent our company and the clients we serve.

As our reputation continues to grow throughout Berks, Lancaster, Lehigh, and Schuylkill Counties and beyond, we are seeking professional security officers who demonstrate strong customer service skills, reliability, and sound security awareness.

We are currently hiring part-time security officers for weekday, evening, overnight, and weekend shifts. At KRE Security, you're not just another number. As a locally owned company, we take pride in building a team of professionals who support each other and provide exceptional service to our clients.

Officers may work at a consistent site or multiple client locations, depending on availability and preference. Whether you have prior security experience or are looking to start a new career, we're willing to train the right candidates.

Employees working 30+ hours per week who successfully complete our 60-day probationary period are eligible for Capital Blue Cross PPO health insurance with minimal employee contribution.
