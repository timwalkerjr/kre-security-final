\[ Traffic Safety Specialists \]

## Professional Traffic Control Across Eastern PA

KRE Security provides traffic control services for Concerts, High School/College sporting events, facility ingress and egress, to assist with traffic moving in and out of businesses, parking garages, churches, events, and more.

Our officers comply with local and state traffic laws to keep traffic flow moving and keep drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians safe. KRE staff retain professional based instruction and certification to provide professional based services for any event across Eastern Pennsylvania.

KRE works alongside Local and State Police in addition to numerous emergency service organizations during our events.

Custom based traffic control service and safety plans are created with client based input and implementation. The safe and systematic parking of vehicles and event traffic management is crucial to the ultimate safety and enjoyment of an event. Done correctly, it will alleviate congestion and frustration, bringing a positive effect and potential for repeat business.
