\[ Wireless Advantage \]

## Why Go Wireless for Your  Home Security System?

\[ Real-Time Awareness \]

Wireless systems are more secure and allow you to have real-time awareness and alerts from your home via your mobile app. These systems are increasingly tamper proof because there are no exposed lines that can be cut from outside.

\[ Remote Monitoring \]

Your wireless system comes with a phone app you can use to monitor surveillance footage live. Check who's at the door, monitor kids in the backyard, or check on pets while at work — from wherever you are.

\[ Easy Expansion \]

As your family grows and evolves, your security needs may as well. It's easy to add new cameras with a wireless system. Let us know what you're looking to protect and we'll help create a system that's just right for you.
