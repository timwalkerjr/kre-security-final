## Our Logistical Security Services

Our very affordable security services relieve your personnel of select inventory and inspection duties while enhancing our ability to provide proper security and accountability of all transportation containers for our clients. Whether you manage a storage yard, loading dock or a rail yard, our management team will design post order duties to match your business's needs and assign guards that are well-qualified to accomplish them.

Inventory Control Assistance

Assisting in accurate management of all transportation container inventories.

Fixed Guard Booth & GPS Tracking

Stationary guard posts combined with GPS vehicle and asset tracking.

Docking Designation

Keeping accurate timing and coordinating proper dock assignments.

Arrival & Departure Logging

Tracking and maintaining logs for the arrival and departure of vehicles, materials, and equipment.

Shipping Inventory Management

Managing and verifying all shipping inventory against manifests.

Full Truck Inspections

Checking driver name, documenting front and back license plates, and ensuring no unauthorized pallets or equipment leave the facility.

Reefer Temperature Logging

For refrigerated trucks, inspecting and logging refrigeration temperatures.

Cal/OSHA Audit Support

Assisting in fulfilling Cal/OSHA audit requirements with highly trained staff.
