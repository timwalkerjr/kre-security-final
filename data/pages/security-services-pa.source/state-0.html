About KRE Security

KRE Security LLC. was founded in August of 1990. We are a full-time Security Service and Investigations Corporation. We provide factually based investigations and conduct every investigation ethically, morally, legally and with the highest degree of integrity.

Our goal is to meet your security needs 24/7 while providing cost effective services to our customers.

[ Owner & President ]

Jarrod Emes

Owner and President

Jarrod is a graduate of Alvernia College, where he earned a Bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice with a Minor in Sociology. In 2007, he was honored with the Saint Michael Archangel Award. Jarrod also served as a "Program Advisory Board Member" for the Criminal Justice Department at Berks Technical Institute.

Jarrod's work experience spans over 20 years in the Emergency Services field, specializing in Fire/Rescue and Emergency Management. He currently oversees municipal hazard plans, mitigation projects, and ongoing safety assessments. His previous roles include working at the Berks County Juvenile Detention Center, the Berks County Department of Emergency Services as a Special Projects Officer, and serving as a Deputy Coroner for the Schuylkill County Coroner's Office.

With over 15 years of experience at KRE Security/Investigations Inc., and 6 years as the proud owner, Jarrod remains deeply committed to client service. As Owner and President, he works directly with clients on a daily basis and is always available 24/7, 365 days a year.
