Safe City, or Smart City, projects are rapidly becoming an ideal way to build an effective and proactive security strategy while allowing increased collaboration between city officials, security leaders, law enforcement, and first responders.

Close partnerships between these parties enable cities to efficiently identify current threats while learning and analyzing data points to be best prepared to address future risks.

## Situational Awareness

Safe City initiatives help cities achieve true situational awareness—both in regular times and during emergency situations—by aggregating a significant amount of data and turning it into actionable intelligence.

## Actionable Intelligence

Correlated information enables officials to quickly and efficiently identify threats, manage emergency situations, and investigate incidents before they escalate.
